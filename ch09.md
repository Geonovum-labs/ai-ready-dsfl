# Findings and preliminary recommendations

<p class="note" title="Voorlopig">
The recommendations in this chapter come from the approach document and its proposals. The manual for reuse by other organisations will only follow after a review of the full series of work sessions ([[APPROACH]], §10). What applies generally and what is specific to geodata has therefore not yet been determined.
</p>

## Start with the why

- First determine the goals and test each proposal against them. The government-wide guide to generative AI recommends always starting from the problem ([[APPROACH]], §5, with reference to [[GENAI GUIDE]]).
- Maintain three levels: one overarching goal, main goals and sub-goals. Policy goals are not included.
- Use two or three measuring points per target and one target value per measuring point per block.
- Treat legal requirements as preconditions, not as goals.

Source: [[APPROACH]], §5.

## Follow the user's demand

- Give each blueprint the user's question as a title, in their own words.
- Build the first blueprint together with the domain experts and then have groups reuse the template.
- Start simple and then move on to more complex questions.
- Determine the current situation by looking at what an assistant is really doing, not by assuming it.

Source: [[APPROACH]], §6.

## Formulate bottlenecks as open-ended questions

- Write one question per bottleneck, without a solution in it.
- Choose the right level: not so narrow that the solution is fixed, not so wide that there is no grip.

Source: [[APPROACH]], §7.

## Involve the right roles

The approach document recommends that in addition to domain experts, advisors for policy, law, information security and privacy should also be involved. In the practical example, this is not yet a fixed part ([[APPROACH]], §8).
